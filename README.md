### &nbsp;&nbsp;Three ideas at the centre

**How we operate.** The whole strategy reduces to three decisions, each made the same way every time.

<br/>

**01 · One-sided positioning**

Conventional liquidity sits on both sides of price and is exposed either way. We place it on one side, around prices chosen in advance — exposure by intention, with the rest held as cash.

<div align="center">
  <img src="./assets/viz-onesided.svg" alt="One-sided positioning — concentrated liquidity on one side of live price, cash held in reserve" width="620">
</div>

<br/>

**02 · Automated range management**

A static position drifts out of range and stops working. Ours is re-centred automatically as the market moves — the same way every time, with no manual intervention.

<div align="center">
  <img src="./assets/viz-recenter.svg" alt="Automated range management — the active range re-centres by rule as price moves" width="620">
</div>

<br/>

**03 · Hedging & risk overlay**

On top of the positions, a risk-management overlay is designed to dampen exposure when conditions turn against us. Its job is to defend capital, not to chase returns.

<div align="center">
  <img src="./assets/viz-riskoverlay.svg" alt="Hedging and risk overlay — net exposure dampened by rule inside a risk gate" width="620">
</div>

### &nbsp;&nbsp;How it works

**01** &nbsp;Capital is commingled in the operator wallet and deployed into one of the active pools.

**02** &nbsp;The system works it — reshapes, fee claims, harvests, and risk gates. Nothing happens at random; nothing happens without a rule.

**03** &nbsp;Every action is logged and NAV refreshes on a fixed snapshot — the same one every investor sees.
